# REQ-012: Analytics PII Pseudonymization & Async `DATA_FEED` Isolation

- **Framework:** `PRIVACY`
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Owner:** `team-data` (`#data-analytics`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-analytics` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-007`

## Privacy & Failure-Domain Isolation Mandate

Business intelligence and revenue funnel reporting on `svc-analytics` (`team-data`, `#data-analytics`) MUST NEVER expose raw customer PII or couple its uptime to synchronous Tier-0 commerce transactions.

## Technical Controls

1. **Pseudonymized Event Ingestion:** Before reaching `api-analytics-ingest-v1`, upstream feeds strip emails, names, phone numbers, and card tokens, retaining only salted irreversible customer cohort hashes and aggregated revenue attributes (`REQ-007`).
2. **Strictly Asynchronous `DATA_FEED` Consumption:** `svc-analytics` consumes non-blocking `DATA_FEED` streams from `svc-orders`, `svc-ledger`, and `svc-inventory` (`is_sync = false`).
3. **Blast-Radius Classification:** Change-impact analyses MUST classify `svc-analytics` as `DATA_FEED_CONSUMER` (non-blocking lag impact) rather than a synchronous OLTP outage dependency, preventing alert fatigue during Tier-0 reviews.
