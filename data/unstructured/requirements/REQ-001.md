# REQ-001: Cardholder Data Tokenization (`tok_pci_v2_<32hex>`)

- **Framework:** `PCI-DSS 4.0`
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL`
- **Owner:** `team-security` (`#security-iam`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-payments-refund-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-checkout-submit-v1` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-payments-charge-v1` (`AT_RISK`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-payments` (`COMPLIANT`), `svc-checkout` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-004`, `ADR-001`

## Regulatory Mandate

Under **PCI-DSS 4.0** Requirement 3.5, Acme Commerce MUST minimize its cardholder data environment (CDE) by prohibiting raw Primary Account Numbers (PANs) in operational commerce services, logs, queues, and analytics stores. All card-present and card-not-present payment flows MUST use opaque vault tokens issued by the isolated tokenization boundary.

## Technical Controls & Verification

1. **Strict Token Format:** Payment APIs (`api-payments-charge-v2`, `api-payments-refund-v2`, `api-checkout-submit-v1`) MUST validate that `payment_token` strictly matches `tok_pci_v2_<32hex>` (`tok_pci_v2_` prefix + 32 lowercase hex characters) per `ADR-004`. Any payload containing a Luhn-valid 13–19 digit raw PAN MUST be rejected with HTTP `400`.
2. **Audit Exception (`AT_RISK`):** Legacy `api-payments-charge-v1` remains `AT_RISK` against `REQ-001` until `svc-billing` completes migration tracked under `REQ-004`.
3. **Continuous DLP Scanning:** Structured audit logs and Cloud Spanner operational columns undergo continuous PII/PAN detection scans managed by `team-security`.
