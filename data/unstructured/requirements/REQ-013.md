# REQ-013: Partner Gateway Per-Tenant Rate Limiting & Quota Isolation

- **Framework:** `SECURITY`
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Owner:** `team-security` (`#security-iam`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-partner-checkout-v1` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-partner-gw` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-006`

## Perimeter Resilience Requirement

External B2B merchant integrations entering through `svc-partner-gw` (`api-partner-checkout-v1`, `api-partner-settlement-v1`) represent untrusted traffic bursts that could otherwise exhaust downstream capacity in `svc-checkout`, `svc-billing`, and `svc-payments`.

## Technical Controls

1. **Per-Tenant Token Bucket Quotas:** `svc-partner-gw` enforces per-partner RPS and burst limits based on verified OIDC tenant claims (`api-auth-token-v2`, `ADR-006`), returning HTTP `429 Too Many Requests` with `Retry-After` headers before forwarding traffic to `svc-checkout` or `svc-billing`.
2. **Payload & Timeout Guardrails:** Request bodies are capped at 256 KiB and partner checkouts inherit strict downstream deadlines (`450ms` p99 on `api-partner-checkout-v1`).
3. **Abuse Telemetry:** Sustained quota violations alert `team-security` (`#security-iam`) and `team-integrations` (`#partner-messaging`).
