# REQ-005: SOX Financial Auditability & 1:1 Idempotent Ledger Posting

- **Framework:** `SOX`
- **Severity:** `CRITICAL`
- **Owner:** `team-billing` (`#billing-ledger`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-payments-refund-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-billing-invoice-v1` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-billing-reconcile-v1` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-ledger-post-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-ledger-balance-v2` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-ledger` (`COMPLIANT`), `svc-billing` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-005`, `ADR-008`, `ADR-002`

## SOX Section 404 Compliance Objective

Under **SOX** (Sarbanes-Oxley Act) internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR), every revenue-recognizing event processed by `svc-payments` and `svc-billing` MUST produce an immutable, balanced, auditable double-entry journal in `svc-ledger` with zero unexplained variance.

## Mandatory Technical Controls

1. **Idempotent 1:1 Posting (`ADR-005`):** Every call to `api-payments-charge-v2`, `api-payments-refund-v2`, `api-billing-invoice-v1`, and `api-ledger-post-v2` MUST supply a unique UUIDv4 `Idempotency-Key` committed atomically alongside ledger journal rows so retries never duplicate accounting entries.
2. **Write-Ahead Double-Entry Integrity (`ADR-008`):** `svc-ledger` enforces `SUM(debits) == SUM(credits)` at write time and prohibits destructive SQL `UPDATE`/`DELETE` mutations on posted journals; adjustments require explicit reversing entries.
3. **Continuous Reconciliation:** `api-billing-reconcile-v1` compares `api-ledger-balance-v2` against processor settlement totals hourly and retains immutable audit evidence for 7 years.
