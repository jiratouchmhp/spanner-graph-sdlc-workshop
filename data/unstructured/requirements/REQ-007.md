# REQ-007: Thailand PDPA Primary Operational Data Residency (`asia-southeast3`)

- **Framework:** `THAILAND_PDPA`
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Owner:** `team-security` (`#security-iam`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-orders-create-v2` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-checkout-submit-v1` (`COMPLIANT`)

## Data Residency Requirement

To comply with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (**PDPA**), primary operational customer records, order histories, tokenized payment state, and double-entry ledger entries MUST be stored and served synchronously inside Bangkok (`asia-southeast3` / Cloud Spanner `regional-asia-southeast3` 3-zone synchronous quorum).

## Cross-Border Controls (Singapore `asia-southeast1`)

1. **Primary Storage Lock:** Production operational databases for `svc-payments`, `svc-orders`, and `svc-ledger` remain pinned to `regional-asia-southeast3` (`asia-southeast3-a/b/c`).
2. **Documented Cross-Jurisdictional Transit:** Vertex AI embedding/LLM endpoints (`gemini-embedding-001`, `gemini-2.5-flash`), Model Armor (`modelarmor.asia-southeast1.rep.googleapis.com`), and encrypted DR backup copies (`REQ-014`) operate in Singapore (`asia-southeast1`, ~25ms RTT). Prompts and embeddings MUST strip direct PII and document PDPA cross-border transfer safeguards in compliance reviews.
