# REQ-009: Checkout Cart-to-Order Saga Compensation Consistency

- **Framework:** `RESILIENCE`
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Owner:** `team-checkout` (`#checkout-orders`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-orders-create-v2` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-checkout` (`COMPLIANT`), `svc-orders` (`COMPLIANT`), `svc-inventory` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-005`, `ADR-009`

## Saga Resilience Requirement

Multi-service checkout flows spanning `svc-orders`, `svc-checkout`, `svc-inventory`, `svc-fraud`, and `svc-payments` MUST maintain eventual consistency without leaked stock reservations or orphaned payment authorizations.

## Compensation Controls

1. **Deterministic Rollback:** If `api-payments-charge-v2` declines (for example on a `> $500` `45ms` fraud gate timeout under `REQ-003`) or `api-orders-create-v2` aborts mid-flight, `svc-checkout` MUST issue a compensating release for the `api-inventory-reserve-v1` hold (`ADR-009`).
2. **TTL Safety Net:** All inventory soft-locks carry a 15-minute TTL so crashed orchestrator workers cannot permanently strand inventory.
3. **Idempotent Retries:** Saga steps propagate the same `Idempotency-Key` (`ADR-005`) so compensation and confirmation workers are strictly idempotent.
