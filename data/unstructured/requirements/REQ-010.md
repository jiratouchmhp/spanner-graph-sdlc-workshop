# REQ-010: At-Least-Once Webhook Delivery & HMAC-SHA256 Signing

- **Framework:** `MESSAGING`
- **Severity:** `MEDIUM`
- **Owner:** `team-integrations` (`#partner-messaging`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-webhooks-emit-v1` (`COMPLIANT`), `api-webhooks-verify-v1` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-webhooks` (`COMPLIANT`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-007`

## Merchant Webhook Reliability & Authenticity

Merchants rely on asynchronous webhook callbacks dispatched by `svc-webhooks` to trigger fulfillment and ERP bookkeeping after payment capture and settlement updates from `svc-orders` and `svc-partner-gw`.

## Required Controls

1. **HMAC-SHA256 Payload Signatures:** Every outbound HTTP request emitted by `api-webhooks-emit-v1` MUST include an `X-Acme-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 digest computed over `(timestamp + "." + raw_body)` using a per-merchant rotated signing secret, verifiable via `api-webhooks-verify-v1`.
2. **At-Least-Once Delivery & DLQ (`ADR-007`):** Non-`2xx` merchant responses retry with exponential backoff up to 8 attempts over 24 hours before landing in the Dead-Letter Queue for operator replay.
3. **Replay Protection:** Merchants validate that webhook timestamps are within a 5-minute tolerance window and deduplicate by `event_id`.
