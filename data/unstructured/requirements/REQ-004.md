# REQ-004: Legacy v1 Payment Charge Endpoint Sunset

- **Framework:** `COMPLIANCE_LEGACY`
- **Severity:** `HIGH`
- **Owner:** `team-payments` (`#payments-eng`)
- **Applicable APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v1` (`AT_RISK`)
- **Applicable Services:** `svc-billing` (`AT_RISK`), `svc-payments` (`IN_PROGRESS`)
- **Related ADRs:** `ADR-001`, `ADR-004`, `ADR-010`

## Sunset Objective

Decommission the `DEPRECATED` `api-payments-charge-v1` (`POST /v1/payments/charge`) endpoint introduced under `ADR-001` so Acme Commerce can eliminate residual raw-PAN handling (`REQ-001`) and bring 100% of card volume onto `api-payments-charge-v2`.

## Current Blockers & Remediation Plan

- **Sole Remaining Caller (`AT_RISK`):** `svc-billing` (`team-billing`, `#billing-ledger`) continues invoking `api-payments-charge-v1` at `85` RPS (`migration_status = AT_RISK`) for legacy annual invoice contracts.
- **Migration Milestones:**
  1. Complete bulk card re-tokenization to `tok_pci_v2_<32hex>` across legacy billing profiles.
  2. Switch `svc-billing` recurring job workers to `api-payments-charge-v2` with UUIDv4 `Idempotency-Key` headers (`ADR-005`).
  3. Verify `0` RPS on `api-payments-charge-v1` for 14 consecutive days, then disable the route with HTTP `410 Gone`.
