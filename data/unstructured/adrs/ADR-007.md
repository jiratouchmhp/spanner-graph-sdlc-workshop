# ADR-007: Asynchronous Event Envelope & Dead-Letter Queue Contract

- **Status:** `ACCEPTED`
- **Owner:** `team-integrations` (`#partner-messaging`)
- **Decision Date:** `2025-01-22`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-notify-dispatch-v1` (`MANDATES`), `api-notify-receipt-v1` (`MANDATES`), `api-webhooks-emit-v1` (`MANDATES`), `api-webhooks-verify-v1` (`CONSTRAINS`), `api-analytics-ingest-v1` (`CONSTRAINS`)

## Context

Asynchronous messaging between `svc-orders`, `svc-notifications`, `svc-webhooks`, and `svc-analytics` previously used ad-hoc JSON schemas with inconsistent retry policies, causing lost merchant webhooks and unattributable delivery loops.

## Decision

1. **Standardized Event Envelope:** Every asynchronous message posted to `api-notify-dispatch-v1`, `api-notify-receipt-v1`, `api-webhooks-emit-v1`, and `api-analytics-ingest-v1` MUST wrap domain payloads in a CloudEvents 1.0 envelope carrying `event_id` (UUIDv4), `source_service`, `event_type`, `occurred_at`, `traceparent` (`REQ-011`), and `schema_version`.
2. **Cycle-Safe Bidirectional Order-Notification Contract:** `svc-orders` emits `order.confirmed` events to `svc-notifications` (`api-notify-dispatch-v1`), and `svc-notifications` emits `notification.delivered` callbacks back to `svc-orders` (`api-notify-receipt-v1` / status sync). Consumers MUST enforce `event_id` deduplication and never re-trigger dispatch on receipt callbacks, preventing infinite event amplification across the intentional `svc-orders <-> svc-notifications` cycle.
3. **Exponential Backoff & Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ):** Failed webhook deliveries (`svc-webhooks`, `REQ-010`) retry 8 times over 24 hours with jitter before routing to a monitored DLQ with automated replay tooling.

## Consequences

- **Decoupled Blast Radius:** Transient outages in `svc-notifications`, `svc-webhooks`, or `svc-analytics` queue safely without blocking synchronous `svc-checkout` or `svc-payments` commits.
- **Graph Traversal Requirement:** Dependency analysis over `svc-orders` and `svc-notifications` MUST use cycle-safe `MATCH TRAIL` semantics.
