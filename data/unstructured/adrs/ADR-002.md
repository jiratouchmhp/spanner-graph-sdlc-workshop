# ADR-002: Nightly Batch Ledger Settlement

- **Status:** `SUPERSEDED` (superseded by `ADR-008` on `2025-02-14`)
- **Owner:** `team-billing` (`#billing-ledger`)
- **Decision Date:** `2023-04-10`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-billing-reconcile-v1` (`DEPRECATED_LEGACY`)

## Context

Prior to 2025, `svc-payments` and `svc-billing` logged completed charges into operational tables while finance accounting waited for a 02:00 UTC nightly batch job to aggregate merchant credits, fees, and tax liabilities into the general ledger.

## Decision

Perform end-of-day batch settlement and reconciliation via `api-billing-reconcile-v1` on `svc-billing` rather than synchronous per-transaction double-entry ledger posting.

## Consequences & Supersession

- **Reconciliation Drift:** Partial failures, duplicate retries, and mid-day refunds caused recurring penny-rounding discrepancies requiring manual finance intervention during SOX quarterly closes (`REQ-005`).
- **Stale Merchant Balances:** Real-time partner payout views lagged by up to 24 hours.
- **Superseded by `ADR-008`:** On `2025-02-14`, `ADR-008` mandated synchronous write-ahead double-entry ledger posting through `svc-ledger` (`api-ledger-post-v2`), relegating `api-billing-reconcile-v1` to an automated audit verification check.
