# ADR-009: Inventory Soft-Lock Reservation & 15-Minute Expiry TTL

- **Status:** `ACCEPTED`
- **Owner:** `team-catalog` (`#catalog-inventory`)
- **Decision Date:** `2025-03-09`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-inventory-reserve-v1` (`MANDATES`), `api-inventory-catalog-v1` (`CONSTRAINS`)

## Context

During flash sales, concurrent checkouts frequently raced on the final units of popular SKUs, resulting in customers getting charged via `api-payments-charge-v2` for out-of-stock items and forcing costly manual refunds.

## Decision

1. **15-Minute Soft-Lock Reservations:** Before initiating payment capture, `svc-checkout` and `svc-orders` MUST acquire an atomic SKU reservation via `api-inventory-reserve-v1` on `svc-inventory`. Each reservation carries a strict 15-minute expiry timestamp (`expires_at = NOW + 15m`).
2. **Saga Compensation on Payment Failure (`REQ-009`):** If `api-payments-charge-v2` declines (for example due to a `> $500` fail-closed fraud timeout) or checkout aborts, `svc-checkout` immediately releases the soft-lock. If the caller crashes before releasing, Cloud Spanner Row Deletion Policy (TTL) reclaims expired holds automatically.
3. **Read-Optimized Catalog Lookups:** `api-inventory-catalog-v1` serves read-only SKU availability projections (`50ms` budget) to `svc-checkout`, `svc-orders`, and `svc-analytics` `DATA_FEED` consumers without blocking reservation locks.

## Consequences

- **Eliminates Overselling:** Hard stock guarantees protect customer trust and reduce refund volume on `api-payments-refund-v2`.
- **Bounded Lock Contention:** Soft-lock TTLs prevent abandoned shopping carts from hoarding scarce inventory indefinitely.
