# ADR-008: Double-Entry Ledger Write-Ahead Posting & Reconciliation

- **Status:** `ACCEPTED` (supersedes `ADR-002` as of `2025-02-14`)
- **Owner:** `team-billing` (`#billing-ledger`)
- **Decision Date:** `2025-02-14`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-ledger-post-v2` (`MANDATES`), `api-ledger-balance-v2` (`MANDATES`), `api-payments-charge-v2` (`CONSTRAINS`), `api-billing-reconcile-v1` (`CONSTRAINS`)

## Context

Nightly batch settlement under `ADR-002` produced SOX audit exceptions (`REQ-005`) whenever payment captures diverged from merchant settlement totals. External auditors required provable 1:1 balanced accounting at commit time.

## Decision

1. **Synchronous Write-Ahead Double-Entry Posting:** Every successful charge on `api-payments-charge-v2` (`svc-payments`) and invoice capture on `svc-billing` MUST synchronously post a balanced double-entry journal (`SUM(debits) == SUM(credits)`) to `svc-ledger` via `api-ledger-post-v2` using the same transaction `Idempotency-Key` (`ADR-005`).
2. **Immutable Append-Only Ledger in Regional Bangkok Spanner:** Ledger entries are stored in `regional-asia-southeast3` Cloud Spanner (`REQ-007`) as immutable append-only rows. Corrections or refunds MUST post compensating reversing entries rather than executing SQL `UPDATE` or `DELETE` statements.
3. **Continuous Automated Reconciliation:** `api-billing-reconcile-v1` runs hourly parity assertions comparing `api-ledger-balance-v2` against processor settlement files, paging `team-billing` (`#billing-ledger`) on any non-zero variance.

## Consequences

- **Zero Accounting Drift:** Real-time balances are immediately consistent for `svc-partner-gw` settlement queries and `svc-analytics` `DATA_FEED` extracts.
- **Strict Latency Budget:** `api-ledger-post-v2` MUST maintain a `60ms` p99 latency budget so `api-payments-charge-v2` stays inside its `180ms` end-to-end SLA (`REQ-002`).
