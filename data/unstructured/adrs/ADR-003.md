# ADR-003: 30-Day Informal API Deprecation Notice

- **Status:** `SUPERSEDED` (superseded by `ADR-010` on `2025-05-01`)
- **Owner:** `team-payments` (`#payments-eng`)
- **Decision Date:** `2023-06-01`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v1` (`DEPRECATED_LEGACY`)

## Context

When Acme Commerce operated fewer than five internal microservices, service owners announced breaking payload changes in `#payments-eng` and `#checkout-orders` with an informal 30-day migration target and no automated consumer verification gate.

## Decision

Allow service teams to deprecate or modify REST JSON fields after posting a 30-day Slack announcement and tagging direct callers in issue trackers.

## Consequences & Supersession

- **Unplanned Downstream Outages:** Indirect upstream consumers (`svc-orders`, `svc-partner-gw`, `svc-webhooks`) and legacy recurring jobs on `svc-billing` repeatedly broke during 30-day cutovers because transitive dependency graphs were invisible.
- **Incomplete v1 Sunset:** Informal notices failed to drive `svc-billing` off `api-payments-charge-v1`, leaving an `AT_RISK` dependency tracked under `REQ-004`.
- **Superseded by `ADR-010`:** On `2025-05-01`, `ADR-010` replaced the 30-day informal process with a mandatory `180-day` breaking-change freeze and dual-version rollout policy for all `Tier-0` APIs including `api-payments-charge-v2`.
