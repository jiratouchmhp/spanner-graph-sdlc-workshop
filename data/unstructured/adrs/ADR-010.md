# ADR-010: Tier-0 API 180-Day Breaking-Change Freeze Policy

- **Status:** `ACCEPTED` (supersedes `ADR-003` as of `2025-05-01`)
- **Owner:** `team-payments` (`#payments-eng`)
- **Decision Date:** `2025-05-01`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v2` (`CONSTRAINS`), `api-checkout-submit-v1` (`CONSTRAINS`)

## Context

Informal 30-day Slack notices under `ADR-003` caused repeated cross-team regressions across B2B partners and recurring billing jobs. Because `Tier-0` APIs such as `api-payments-charge-v2` sit at the root of multi-hop revenue pipelines (`svc-checkout`, `svc-billing`, `svc-orders`, `svc-partner-gw`, `svc-notifications`, `svc-webhooks`, `svc-analytics`), breaking contract modifications require formal blast-radius analysis and extended migration lead times.

## Decision

1. **Mandatory 180-Day Freeze Window (`REQ-008`):** No backward-incompatible request, response, header, error-code, or SLA change may be deployed to any `Tier-0` API — explicitly including `api-payments-charge-v2` (`POST /v2/payments/charge`) and `api-checkout-submit-v1` — without a formal **180-day** advance deprecation notice and freeze period.
2. **Dual-Version Parallel Rollout:** Any breaking evolution MUST ship under a new versioned path (for example `/v3/...`) running in parallel alongside the existing `Tier-0` version for at least `180-day` duration while telemetry confirms `0` RPS on the retiring version.
3. **Graph Blast-Radius Sign-Off:** Before Architecture Review Board approval, the owning team (`team-payments` for `api-payments-charge-v2`) MUST generate a transitive blast-radius report identifying every direct synchronous caller (`svc-checkout`, `svc-billing`), transitive upstream service (`svc-orders`, `svc-partner-gw`, `svc-notifications`, `svc-webhooks`), non-blocking `DATA_FEED` consumer (`svc-analytics`), and legacy `AT_RISK` debt (`svc-billing` on `api-payments-charge-v1`), obtaining written sign-off from each owning team's escalation lead.

## Consequences

- **Predictable Change Governance:** Eliminates surprise breaking changes on `Tier-0` revenue-critical APIs and aligns enterprise partners (`team-integrations`) on quarterly roadmap windows.
- **Mandatory Knowledge Graph Integration:** Change reviews rely directly on the Acme Commerce SDLC Knowledge Graph to guarantee zero missed transitive consumers before proposing any modification to `api-payments-charge-v2`.
