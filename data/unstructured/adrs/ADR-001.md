# ADR-001: Legacy Direct PAN Payment Processing (v1)

- **Status:** `SUPERSEDED` (superseded by `ADR-004` on `2024-09-12`)
- **Owner:** `team-payments` (`#payments-eng`)
- **Decision Date:** `2023-02-15`
- **Governed APIs:** `api-payments-charge-v1` (`DEPRECATED_LEGACY`)

## Context

In early 2023, Acme Commerce launched `svc-payments` with `api-payments-charge-v1` (`POST /v1/payments/charge`) accepting raw 16-digit Primary Account Numbers (PANs), CVV codes, and card expiration dates directly in JSON request payloads. This design accelerated initial merchant onboarding but expanded the PCI-DSS cardholder data environment (CDE) audit boundary to every upstream service handling checkout or recurring billing.

## Decision

Accept encrypted-in-transit raw PAN payloads directly on `api-payments-charge-v1` within `svc-payments`, passing card payloads synchronously to external acquiring processors without an isolated tokenization vault or deterministic fraud-scoring SLA boundary.

## Consequences & Supersession

- **Expanded PCI-DSS Scope:** Upstream callers such as `svc-billing` were pulled into PCI-DSS 4.0 CDE scope, increasing audit cost and breach blast radius.
- **No Fail-Closed Risk Gate:** Early v1 flows lacked a strict `45ms` fraud scoring timeout or `> $500` fail-closed decline rule.
- **Superseded by `ADR-004`:** On `2024-09-12`, `ADR-004` replaced raw PAN handling with `tok_pci_v2_<32hex>` tokenization on `api-payments-charge-v2`. `api-payments-charge-v1` is now `DEPRECATED`, and `svc-billing` remains the final `AT_RISK` legacy caller tracked for retirement under `REQ-004`.
